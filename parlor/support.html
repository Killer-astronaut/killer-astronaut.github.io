Support

Parlor is in development. For questions, testing notes, or support, email hello@getlokei.com.

Is Parlor available yet?

Not publicly. Parlor is still being playtested before a store release.

What kind of game is it?

Parlor is a table game about answering prompts, reading the room, and catching bluffs.

Does it need an account?

No. The product direction is local play without a developer account or player profile.

Why does nearby play need testing?

Nearby table discovery depends on real devices, Wi-Fi, and local network behavior, so it needs real-room testing.
