PARLOR IS IN DEVELOPMENT

A pass-and-play table game about wild answers, sharp reads, and catching the bluff before the room buys it.

Current focus

Playtesting quick games, nearby tables, voting, reveal flow, and table feel.

What is being built

A compact party-game loop.

Parlor is being shaped around real people at one table, with local-first gameplay and nearby play still under test.

01 - Quick Game

One phone, one table.

Pass the phone, answer prompts, call truth or lie, and review the table ledger.

02 - Classic Table

Host and join nearby.

Nearby hosting is being tested for real rooms, table codes, and local network reliability.

03 - Reads

Vote on the tell.

Answer cards stay hidden until the right phase so the room has something to deduce.

04 - Privacy

No developer account.

Parlor is designed without accounts, ads, analytics, or developer-run player profiles.
