01 · Care

Feed, play, and rest.

Keep hunger, happiness, and energy in balance with a small Tamagotchi-style care loop designed for quick desktop breaks.

02 · Call

Three taps, if you want them.

Tap call is off by default. If enabled, audio is processed on your Mac only to detect three quick taps.

03 · Personalize

Use your own pet photo.

Import a user-selected image, rename your companion, or remove the photo any time from Settings.

No account. No feed.

Cat Caller has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-run service collecting app data.
