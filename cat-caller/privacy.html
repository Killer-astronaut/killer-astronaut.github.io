Privacy Policy

Last updated July 7, 2026

Cat Caller does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertisements, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Microphone access

Tap call is optional and off by default. If enabled, Cat Caller uses microphone input only to detect three quick taps near your Mac. Audio is processed locally on your Mac and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Pet photos

If you choose a custom pet photo, the selected image is copied into Cat Caller's local app container on your Mac. You can remove the photo from Settings at any time. Cat Caller does not upload pet photos.

Data stored on your Mac

Cat Caller stores preferences, pet-care state, pet name, and achievement progress locally using system storage such as UserDefaults and the app container. This information stays on your Mac.

Network access

Cat Caller does not use a developer-run server for microphone audio, pet photos, settings, or virtual-pet state.

Children

Because Cat Caller collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@getlokei.com.
