App support

Support

Cat Caller is designed to work without an account or setup. For anything not covered below, email hello@getlokei.com.

How do I open Cat Caller?

Launch the app, then use the Cat Caller menu-bar icon to open the main care window, summon the floating companion, or open Settings.

Why does Cat Caller ask for microphone access?

Microphone access is only used for optional tap call. If you enable it, Cat Caller listens locally for three quick taps near your Mac. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Can I use the app without microphone access?

Yes. Tap call is off by default, and the virtual pet, menu-bar controls, floating companion, and pet-photo features work without enabling microphone access.

How do I turn tap call off?

Open Settings from the Cat Caller menu-bar icon, then turn off “Listen for three quick taps.”

Where are pet photos stored?

User-selected pet photos are copied into the app container on your Mac. You can remove a photo from Settings at any time.

Does Cat Caller collect analytics or show ads?

No. Cat Caller has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or third-party tracking.

How do I reset permissions?

Open macOS System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Microphone. You can change Cat Caller's microphone permission there.
