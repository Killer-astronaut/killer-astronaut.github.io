Privacy Policy

Last updated June 16, 2026

Borrowed Time does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertisements, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Network access

The app makes anonymous requests to the U.S. Treasury's public FiscalData API at api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov to retrieve national debt records. Borrowed Time does not add personal information, advertising identifiers, or app-specific user identifiers to those requests.

Data stored on your device

The app stores its preferences and the last successfully downloaded Treasury snapshot and history locally on your device. This allows the last verified record to remain visible if the network is unavailable. That information is not sent to the developer or shared with third parties.

In-app purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Borrowed Time does not receive your payment details. Tips unlock nothing and are never required.

External links and photographs

Source links open websites in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies. Monthly archive photographs are public-domain or no-known-restrictions images from institutions including the National Archives, Library of Congress, NASA, and National Park Service. They are bundled with the app and credited in context.

Children

Because Borrowed Time collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.
