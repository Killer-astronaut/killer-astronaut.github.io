Privacy Policy

Last updated July 7, 2026

Lokei Beats is being built without developer accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Local music library

User-imported tracks, playlists, metadata, artwork, playback state, and tips state are intended to stay on the user's device unless the user chooses a sharing action.

Open music discovery

Open music discovery may connect to public music or metadata sources to show permissively licensed releases and attribution. Those sources have their own policies.

Nearby sharing

Nearby sharing uses local device-to-device transfer so users can offer or receive tracks they have the right to share. Lokei Beats does not operate a developer-run music sharing server.

In-app purchases

Optional tips, if enabled, are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tips are optional and do not unlock core features.

Data collected by developer

None. Lokei Beats does not collect personal music libraries, listening history, analytics, advertising identifiers, or account data.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@getlokei.com.
