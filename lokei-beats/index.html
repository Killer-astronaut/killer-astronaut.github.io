What is being built

Music that stays close to the hardware.

Lokei Beats is being tested as a small music app for local files, open music discovery, and person-to-person sharing with clear rights guidance.

01 - Library

Bring your own tracks.

Import audio files and organize playlists without a developer-run music account.

02 - Open Music

Discover permissive releases.

Open music discovery is focused on public-domain and permissively licensed releases with attribution.

03 - Nearby

Share responsibly.

Nearby sharing is being tested for music you created, own, or have permission to distribute.

04 - Private

No ads or subscription.

The app direction is local-first, account-free, and supported by optional tips rather than required subscriptions.
