Support

Lokei Beats is in development. For questions, testing notes, or support, email hello@getlokei.com.

Is Lokei Beats available yet?

Not publicly. Lokei Beats is still being tested on Mac and iOS with real music files and hardware.

What music can I share?

Only share music you created, own, or have permission to distribute, including public-domain and permissively licensed tracks.

Does it require an account?

No. The product direction is local-first, account-free music management.

What needs testing?

Import, playback, artwork, playlist editing, CD/removable media, nearby sharing, and iOS file handling.
