Classrooms

A patient tutor for the whole class, hosted on the teacher's device. It guides students toward answers instead of handing them over — and runs quiz rounds with hidden answers it checks and scores. No student data ever leaves the room.

Game nights

An AI game master that runs trivia, story adventures, and party games — keeping score by name and keeping everyone in. Hidden-answer rounds mean nobody can copy; the reveal lands for everyone at once.

Work sessions

One private AI in the meeting — legal, medical, and engineering room modes with honest, professional framing. Capture decisions, tasks, and files into a shared workspace that never touches a server.

Join in seconds

The host shows a QR code or a six-digit room code — classmates and friends are in within seconds. The host approves who gets in (or lets the code auto-admit a whole class at once).

People-first chat

Talk to each other like any group chat — the AI stays quiet unless you mention @lokei or ask it directly. Hosts can switch on private messages between members, with everyone told when the rules change.

Local by law, not promise

Rooms are discoverable only on your Wi‑Fi, every connection is TLS-encrypted with a key derived from the room code, and the AI answers on the host's own hardware. There is no server to trust — none exists.

Lokei AI for iPhone & iPad — available now

Pocket-sized. Same privacy.

Now on the App Store — free

Lokei AI is a full standalone chat app — not a remote control. On supported iPhones it runs Apple Intelligence on-device. Anywhere else, it streams from the world's most powerful open-source models running on your own Mac, over your own Wi-Fi. Your prompts never touch a Lokei server.

On-device with Apple Intelligence

On iPhone 15 Pro, the iPhone 16 family, and the iPhone 17 family, Lokei AI taps Apple's on-device foundation model. Zero network. Instant cold-start. Nothing leaves your phone.

Your Mac, as your server

Point Lokei AI at the Ollama or LM Studio server on your Mac and your iPhone gets unlimited access to 70B-class open-source models — Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma — without any cloud, subscription, or rate limit.

Local-network by default

Out of the box the address bar accepts only localhost, .local, and private Wi-Fi addresses — public URLs are rejected. Pointing Lokei AI anywhere beyond your network takes a deliberate opt-in, and the built-in Privacy Audit always shows which mode you're in.

Advisor mode

Run a fast small model on your phone for everyday questions. When the on-device model sounds unsure, Lokei AI quietly asks a larger model on your Mac for a second opinion and shows that answer instead. The trade-off between speed and depth, finally automatic.

On-device dictation

Speak naturally — Lokei AI uses iOS speech recognition in on-device-only mode. Your audio is never uploaded, not even to Apple.

Attachments & export

Attach photos, screenshots, PDFs, or code. Export any conversation to PDF, share it through the system share sheet, or scrub it with one tap.

Host a Room from your pocket

Rooms works both ways on iPhone and iPad — join a Room someone else is hosting, or host one yourself so everyone nearby shares your device's AI over your own Wi-Fi. No Mac required.

Setup

Up and running in minutes.

1

Choose your backend

On supported Macs, start with Apple Intelligence for private on-device responses. Prefer open-source local models? Choose Ollama instead.

2

Install Lokei

Download Lokei Studio from the Mac App Store, or Lokei AI for iPhone & iPad from the App Store. Onboarding guides you through Apple Intelligence or Ollama setup based on what your device supports.

3

Pick a model if needed

Apple Intelligence needs no model download inside Lokei. For Ollama, open Settings → AI & Models to browse and download local models rated for your Mac's RAM.

4

Start asking

Click the Lokei leaf in your menu bar and start chatting. Local and on-device backends keep your work on your Mac; remote providers are only used if you configure one.
