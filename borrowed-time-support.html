Support

Borrowed Time is designed to work without setup or an account. For a problem not covered below, email jipisjp@hotmail.com.

Where does the debt number come from?

The U.S. Treasury's public Debt to the Penny dataset. The app displays the date of the latest official record and links to the primary source.

Why is the counter marked “estimate”?

Treasury publishes official records on business days rather than every second. Between releases, Borrowed Time applies a short local estimate based on recent reported movement. The estimate is labeled and stops after four days.

What happens when I am offline?

After one successful refresh, the app can show the most recently saved Treasury record and history. Saved data is clearly marked and stays only on your device.

How do I inspect a date on the chart?

Choose a range, then touch and drag across the chart. Borrowed Time shows the corresponding date and official Treasury value.

Does the app collect analytics or show ads?

No. Borrowed Time has no account, ad network, analytics SDK, or personal-data collection.

What does a tip unlock?

Nothing. Tips are optional consumable purchases that support continued development. The full app remains available without one.

How do I report an incorrect value?

Refresh the app and compare the record date with the Treasury dataset. Include the displayed date and value when emailing support.
