Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

The developer of Lokei Fit does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs, and there is no developer-run server. What Lokei Fit knows about you stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch and in your own iCloud account, as described below — and, only if you choose to compete with friends, your streak goes to Apple's Game Center; only if you turn on the weather, an approximate location goes to Apple Weather; and only if you send a story you wrote, it goes to whoever you send it to.

Apple Health

With your permission, Lokei Fit reads from Apple Health the distance you walk and run, your exercise minutes, your heart rate, and your date of birth. Your date of birth is used only to estimate your maximum heart rate, so the game can tell a hard effort from an easy one. Lokei Fit uses this information on your devices to turn the distance you cover into story chapters, in-game money and progress.

With your permission, Lokei Fit also saves the chases you run on Apple Watch to Apple Health as workouts, with their distance and active energy, so they count towards your Activity rings and in other apps.

With your permission, Lokei Fit also reads last night's sleep and the food and water you log, in this game or any other app. A full night's sleep and food logged each give you a small advantage in the game, and the crew notice when you have been drinking water. You can choose which device's sleep counts. Water and food you log in Lokei Fit are saved to Apple Health, so your other apps see them too. If you set yourself a daily calorie limit, it is kept with your settings.

Health data is never sent to the developer, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud. It stays on your devices, with one exception that is yours to make: if you turn on competing with friends, your streak — a count of days — is posted to Game Center, as described below.

Motion

During a chase on iPhone, Lokei Fit uses the phone's step counter to follow your pace as it happens. This stays on your iPhone.

Your weather (optional)

The weather in the game is off until you say yes — on the card on the character screen, or in Settings, under Weather. When it is on, Lokei Fit asks your device for an approximate location (never a precise one), sends it to Apple Weather to ask what the sky is doing where you are, and draws that weather in the game. A run in rain or snow pays more in the game.

Your location is used for that request and nothing else. Lokei Fit never stores it, never puts it in your game or in iCloud, and never sends it to the developer or to anyone but Apple Weather. The weather it reads is held only while the app is running. Apple Weather is Apple's service, and what it receives is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Whether you turned the weather on is one of your settings, and is saved with your game. You can turn it off at any time in the app, or stop Lokei Fit using your location in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Location Services.

Talking to the confidants (iPhone)

Each story has somebody you can talk to — Lou, the ferryman, ECHO. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, their replies are written by Apple Intelligence on your phone itself; on other phones, they answer from lines written for them. Either way, nothing you say to them is saved, put in iCloud, or sent to the developer or anyone else, and the conversation ends when you close it. If you choose to report a reply, your own mail app sends it, and only what you choose to send.

Stories you write and share (iPhone)

With Story Maker you can write stories of your own for the game. They are kept on your iPhone, apart from your game — not in the copy of your game in iCloud, and never sent to the developer. The pen name you write under is kept on your iPhone. The first time you write anything, Lokei Fit also makes a random writer number on your iPhone; it is not linked to your Apple Account, your name, or anything else about you.

A story leaves your iPhone only if you send it. When you send one to a friend, it goes as a file through your iPhone's share sheet — AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or wherever you choose — to whoever you send it to, and nowhere else. The file holds the story, your pen name and your writer number, so that whoever receives it can block stories from you if they want to. Nothing else about you goes with it. Once you have sent a story, it is in the hands of the person you sent it to.

When somebody sends you a story, Lokei Fit shows who it is from before anything is added, and adds it only if you say so. Stories are checked for words that are not allowed in stories people send each other — when they are written, and again when they arrive — and a story that fails is not sent, or not added. Stories you receive are kept on your iPhone, and you can throw any of them away. You can block a writer, which removes their stories and stops any more being added; the writers you block are listed on your iPhone only.

How far you have read a story somebody wrote, and the replies and choices you made in it, are saved with your game, like the game's own stories, and so are in the copy of your game in your iCloud account. The stories themselves are not.

If a story should not have been sent to you, you can report it. Your own mail app sends the report to hello@getlokei.com, with the story attached so that it can be read — and only what you choose to send. Reports are used to keep stories in Lokei Fit safe, and for nothing else.

Your game, and where it is kept

Your game — story progress, the choices and replies you have made, your crew, your garage, in-game money, your settings, and the totals worked out from Apple Health — is saved on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Your iPhone and Apple Watch pass your game directly to each other so they stay in step.

So that you keep your progress if you replace or lose a device, Lokei Fit also keeps a copy of your game in your own iCloud account, using iCloud key-value storage. That copy contains no Health data: no distances, no exercise minutes, no heart rate and no streaks. Each story keeps only its share of your walking, as a proportion, and everything else is rebuilt from Apple Health on the device you restore to. It does hold the game's own records — the chapters you have opened, the fights you have won, the items you have found and used, the streak milestones you have reached, the days you opened the game, and the streak restores you have bought — which say what happened in the game, not what your body did. The developer has no access to your iCloud account.

Competing with friends (optional)

Competing with friends is off until you turn it on, from the Streak screen. When it is on, Lokei Fit signs you in to Apple's Game Center and posts two whole numbers to its leaderboards: your current streak and your best streak, each a number of days. Nothing else is sent — no distances, times, routes, places or heart rate. The leaderboards show these numbers under your Game Center name to your Game Center friends, and to other players who view them.

You can stop at any time with Stop Sharing My Streak. Nothing more is sent after that. Today's leaderboard clears itself every day; your best streak stays in Game Center. Game Center is Apple's service, and what it holds is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing your streak card — the picture of your streak — only happens when you choose to share it, and it goes only where you send it.

Purchases

Lokei Fit sells one optional item: a streak restore, which mends a streak that has ended. It never buys distance, chapters or anything else that walking earns. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store, and Lokei Fit never sees your payment details. Each restore is recorded in your game save by its App Store transaction number, so it is counted exactly once on each of your devices.

Notifications

Notifications — a new chapter, a message from your crew, an evening invitation, a reminder on an evening a walk or opening the game keeps your streak, a weekly recap — are scheduled by the app on your own device. None of them come from a server. You can turn any of them off in the app, or all of them in Settings.

Network access

Lokei Fit uses no developer-run server. The only services it talks to are Apple's: iCloud for the copy of your game, the App Store if you buy a restore, Game Center if you choose to compete with friends, and Apple Weather if you turn on the weather.

Your choices

You can change or withdraw Lokei Fit's access to Apple Health at any time in the Health app, under Sharing, then Apps. If you withdraw it, you keep everything you have already earned, but new walking cannot be counted until access is given back. You can turn the weather off in the app, or withdraw location access in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Location Services. You can throw away any story you wrote or were sent, and block or unblock writers, in the app. Deleting Lokei Fit from a device removes the game, and the stories, saved on that device.

Children

Because the developer collects no personal information, Lokei Fit does not knowingly collect information from children. Purchases and Game Center follow the restrictions a parent sets with Screen Time and Ask to Buy. Stories from friends are checked for words that are not allowed, shown before they are added, and can be reported and their writers blocked, as described above.

Changes
