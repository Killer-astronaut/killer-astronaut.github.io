Support

Lokei Fit needs no account. For anything not covered below, email hello@getlokei.com.

How is my walking counted?

Every kilometre your iPhone or Apple Watch records in Apple Health counts, whether or not Lokei Fit is open. Open the app and the distance is already there — as story chapters, in-game money and progress.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing you have earned is taken away. Chapters open on the distance you cover and the days you turn up, not on streaks. A streak counts the days you walk a kilometre and open the game; miss one and it starts again, unless you restore it within two days, but your money, chapters, plated rides and crew stay exactly where they are. When you come back, the crew is glad to see you.

What does a streak do?

Every day in a row adds 2% to what your walking earns, up to +50% at 25 days, and 7, 30, 100 and 365 days each pay a one-time reward. Only days you actually walked count towards them.

A day counts when you walk a kilometre and open Lokei Fit that day, on your iPhone or your Apple Watch. Walking with the app closed still counts as distance; the day just needs a look in. On an evening you haven't opened it, your crew will remind you, unless you turn Streak Reminder off in Settings.

Keep a streak going for 100 days and your story's finest ride is cast in bronze, then silver at 200, gold at 300, white gold at 365 and platinum at 500. Plated rides are earned in every story at once, kept for good, and never for sale. Plated, in the menu on the character screen, shows how close each one is.

What are the fights?

The last chapter of each act waits behind a turn-based fight against what that act was about, and it opens with distance like any chapter. The fight is made of your stats: every kilometre you have ever walked is your strength, your week's exercise is your stamina, hard effort lands harder blows, and respect makes your crew's help count for more. Every 2 km (1.2 miles) you walk finds something for your pack — to mend with, to strike with, to shield with, or to buy a turn. A fight spends only what you choose to use from your pack. Lose, and you can go again whenever you like; win, and the next chapter opens, with a bounty the first time.

What is a streak restore?

An optional purchase that mends a streak of three days or more that has ended — within two days of the day it ended. You can also keep one in reserve, and it is used by itself the next time a streak ends. A restore only mends a streak; it never buys distance, chapters or anything else that walking earns. Purchases go through the App Store, and Ask to Buy and Screen Time apply as usual.

How do I compete with friends?

Open your streak from the character screen and choose Compete With Friends. Lokei Fit signs you in to Game Center and posts two numbers — your current streak and your best — to its leaderboards, where your Game Center friends can see them next to theirs. Nothing else is sent. You can stop at any time with Stop Sharing My Streak. Share My Streak sends a picture of your streak wherever you choose.

Why does Lokei Fit ask for access to Apple Health?

It reads the distance you walk and run, your exercise minutes, your heart rate, and your date of birth (only to estimate your maximum heart rate). On Apple Watch, it saves the chases you run as workouts. None of this leaves your devices except as described in the privacy policy.

How do I get it on my Apple Watch?

Install Lokei Fit on your iPhone, and the watch app arrives on your paired Apple Watch. If it does not appear on its own, open the Watch app on your iPhone and install Lokei Fit from Available Apps.

Do my iPhone and Apple Watch share one game?

Yes. They pass your game to each other and stay in step, and a copy in your own iCloud account — with no Health data in it — keeps your progress safe if you replace a device.

Can I read a story again?

Yes. Each story has more than one way through. When you reach an ending, choose Read It Again at the bottom of the story. Your money, garage, crew and every kilometre stay exactly where they are; only the chapters reset, and the choices you did not make are there to find.

How do I change Health access or notifications?

Health access is in the Health app, under Sharing, then Apps. Notifications — the evening invitation, the weekly recap, crew news and welcome-back messages — can each be turned off in Lokei Fit's settings.

Does Lokei Fit collect analytics or show ads?

No. There is no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or third-party tracking. Lokei Fit has one optional purchase — the streak restore — and no ads, subscriptions or paid chapters.
