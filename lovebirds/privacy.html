Privacy Policy

Last updated July 7, 2026

LoveBirds is being built without developer accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Partner and vibe data

Partners, vibes, recent history, and widget or Watch state are intended to stay on the user's devices and Apple system storage used by the app.

Messages

When a user sends a vibe through Messages, that message is handled by Apple Messages and the people the user chooses to contact. LoveBirds does not operate a messaging server.

Watch and widgets

Watch and widget features use Apple system frameworks to show or sync the user's own LoveBirds state.

Data collected by developer

None. LoveBirds does not collect contacts, location, photos, microphone audio, camera data, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@getlokei.com.
