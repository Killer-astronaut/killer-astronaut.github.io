Support

LoveBirds is in development. For questions, testing notes, or support, email hello@getlokei.com.

Is LoveBirds available yet?

Not publicly. LoveBirds is still being tested across iPhone, Messages, widgets, and Apple Watch.

Does it need an account?

No. The product direction avoids developer accounts and social profiles.

How are vibes sent?

The iPhone app and Messages extension use Apple Messages and app links rather than a developer-run messaging server.

What should testers focus on?

Partner setup, Messages links, recent history, widget updates, Watch sync, and privacy copy.
