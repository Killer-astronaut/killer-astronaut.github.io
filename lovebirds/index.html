What is being built

Tiny signals for close people.

LoveBirds is being tested as a private, low-friction way to send small signals without building a social network around them.

01 - Partners

Keep a small circle.

Add close people, choose a vibe, and keep recent signals visible without a public feed.

02 - Messages

Send through Apple Messages.

Sharing uses Messages and deep links rather than a developer-run messaging server.

03 - Watch

Log quick moments.

Apple Watch flows are being tested for local haptics and quick history logging.

04 - Private

No ads or tracking.

The product direction avoids accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, and developer data collection.
