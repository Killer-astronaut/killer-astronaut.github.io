Built around what debt-clock users ask for

Trust first. Context second. Spectacle last.

Borrowed Time keeps the urgency of a live counter without sacrificing source clarity, useful history, or a calm reading experience.

01 · Verifiable

The receipt is always visible.

Every headline number starts with the U.S. Treasury's Debt to the Penny dataset. The app labels the official record date, official daily change, and any between-release estimate separately.

02 · Historical

Zoom out before drawing conclusions.

Switch between 30-day, 90-day, one-year, and five-year views. Touch the chart to inspect the actual Treasury value for a specific date.

03 · Personal

Translate trillions into scale.

Move from total debt to public and intragovernmental holdings, per-person and household shares, and fiscal-year interest.

04 · Glanceable

A useful widget, not another ad slot.

Check the number from the Home Screen. No account, advertising SDK, behavioral analytics, or subscription stands between you and the data.

Primary source

Numbers from the people keeping the ledger.

The app reads the public FiscalData API from the U.S. Department of the Treasury. Successful records are saved on-device so the last verified figure remains available when the network does not.
