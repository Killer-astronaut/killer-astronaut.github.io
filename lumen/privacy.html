Privacy Policy

Last updated July 7, 2026

Lumen is being built without developer accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Imported documents

User-selected PDFs, extracted text, chapter data, covers, and playback progress are intended to stay in the app's local library on the user's device.

OCR and playback

OCR and text extraction are designed to run on device when needed. Spoken playback uses Apple system speech voices and system audio behavior.

Voice samples

If custom voice samples are added, they are stored locally for organizing the user's cast and can be removed by the user.

Data collected by developer

None. Lumen does not use a developer-run cloud library for documents, extracted text, voice samples, or playback progress.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@getlokei.com.
