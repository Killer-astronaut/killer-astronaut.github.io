Support

Lumen is in development. For questions, testing notes, or support, email hello@getlokei.com.

Is Lumen available yet?

Not publicly. Lumen is still going through real-device import, OCR, playback, and storage testing.

What files does it use?

The app is being built around user-selected PDFs imported from Files.

Does Lumen upload my PDFs?

No. The product direction is local library storage and on-device document processing.

Why is real-device testing important?

PDF import, OCR, background audio, interruptions, and storage behavior can differ across real devices and files.
